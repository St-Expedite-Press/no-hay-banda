# Template: Carousel Intro (First Slide)

**Objective:** Open a multi-slide carousel that walks through one film's representation profile by category. The first slide must earn the swipe.

**Structure:** The first slide copy appears as the caption on the carousel post. It should set up the arc — tease what the following slides will show without giving everything away.

**Required inputs:**
- Film title and year
- The number of category dimensions covered in the carousel (2–5)
- One teaser observation that is surprising or counterintuitive
- The film's newshow.biz URL (for the final slide CTA)

**Skeleton:**

```
[Film title, year]. [Teaser observation — what surprised us in the data. 1 sentence. No advocacy.]

[n] dimensions. Swipe for the breakdown.

newshow.biz/movies/[film-slug]

#[genre] #[dimension or discovery tag]
```

**Notes:**
- The teaser observation should be the most counterintuitive or unexpected finding from the film's profile — not the most expected
- Each subsequent slide should cover one category dimension with its score label and a 1–2 sentence observation
- The final slide should include the full newshow.biz URL and a save/share CTA

**Prohibitions:**
- No em dash
- No fabricated data
- Kakusu Protocol on all slides
- Do not claim scores are official
