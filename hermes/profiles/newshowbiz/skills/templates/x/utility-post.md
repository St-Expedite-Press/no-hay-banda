# Template: Utility Post

**Objective:** Promote a specific browse filter or search capability on newshow.biz. Drive qualified traffic to the browse surface rather than a single movie page.

**Character limit:** 280

**Required inputs:**
- The specific filter or search feature being promoted
- An accurate count or range of results it surfaces (must be verifiable from the live site)
- The filter URL or browse URL
- Optional: one example film that appears in the results

**Skeleton:**

```
Looking for [specific audience need]? [Filter description] on newshow.biz surfaces [n] titles [brief characterization of what they share].

[browse URL or filter URL]
```

**Variant (with film example):**

```
Looking for [audience need]? [Film title] is one of [n] titles on newshow.biz [characterization]. Start there or browse the full set.

[newshow.biz URL]
```

**Anti-fabrication rule:** The result count must be verifiable. If the exact count is unknown, use a conservative range or omit it rather than fabricate a number.

**Prohibitions:**
- No em dash
- No fabricated counts or filter results
- Hashtags optional — only if the filter maps to a clear search term audience
