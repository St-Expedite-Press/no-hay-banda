# Template: Thread Breakdown

**Objective:** Explain one film's representation profile across a single category dimension. Drive clicks to the category breakdown on the movie detail page.

**Structure:** 3 posts. Each post is 280 chars max.

**Required inputs:**
- Film title and year
- One specific category (LGBTQ+, Gender, Racial & Ethnic, Disability, or Religious & Cultural)
- The category score and at least one written observation from the movie's breakdown page
- The film's newshow.biz URL
- Category detail URL if available (movie-page anchor or filter URL)

**Skeleton:**

```
Post 1 (hook):
[Film title, year] treats [category aspect] as [cinematic structural observation — not a political frame]. [1–2 sentences. No advocacy.]

Post 2 (breakdown):
[Specific category score label] — [what the score reflects in the film's actual scenes, casting, or narrative architecture. 2–3 sentences. Grounded in the page data.]

Post 3 (CTA):
Full profile + where to watch: [newshow.biz/movies/film-slug]

#[category-relevant hashtag] #[optional second]
```

**Prohibitions:**
- No em dash in any post
- Never state the score as if it is an official industry rating
- Never fabricate specific scenes, character names, or dialogue not cited in source data
- Kakusu Protocol applies to all three posts
- Hashtags only on the final post
