# Template: Original Discovery Post

**Objective:** Introduce one film from the newshow.biz catalog. Drive clicks to the movie detail page.

**Character limit:** 280 (X hard limit — count characters including link and hashtags)

**Hashtag ceiling:** 2

**Required inputs:**
- Film title and year
- At least one specific score or category observation from the movie's newshow.biz page
- The film's newshow.biz URL
- One browse-filter or catalog link (optional — for CTA variation)

**Skeleton:**

```
[Cinematic observation grounded in the film's actual scores or category data — 1 sentence, no advocacy framing]

[newshow.biz/movies/film-slug]

#[genre or dimension hashtag] #[optional second hashtag]
```

**Worked example (do not copy — generate fresh from source data):**

```
"Tár" earned a top-tier gender score not by checking boxes but by centering a woman's wielded authority rather than her suffering. Worth the runtime.

newshow.biz/movies/tar-2022

#drama #film
```

**Prohibitions:**
- No em dash
- No fabricated plot points or character details not sourced from the movie page
- No "score of X/100 certified by..." framing — scores are analytical observations, not official ratings
- No loaded advocacy terms (see Kakusu Protocol)
- Do not lead with an emoji
- Do not exceed 2 hashtags
- Do not duplicate copy from a thread-breakdown post for the same film in the same day
